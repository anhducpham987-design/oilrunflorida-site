Not every job is a big one. Engine and cabin air filters, burnt-out headlights and bulbs, and worn wiper blades are quick fixes, but on many newer cars the headlight bulb or cabin filter is harder to reach than it should be. Oilrun handles them at your home or workplace in Pensacola or Pace, and we're glad to add them onto a larger repair while we're already there.

What to look for

  • Engine air filter: less power or worse fuel economy, or a filter that looks gray and packed with debris.
  • Cabin air filter: weak airflow from the vents, a musty smell, or a dusty film on the inside of the windshield.
  • Headlights and bulbs: one headlight out, a dim or yellowed beam, or a brake, tail, or turn-signal bulb that's failed (a fast-flashing turn signal is the usual clue).
  • Wipers: streaking, chattering, or blades that skip, which is a real safety issue in a Florida downpour.

What we do on-site

We bring the correct filter, bulb, or blade for your year, make, and model and install it on the spot. For headlights that includes vehicles where the bumper cover or inner fender liner has to come off to reach the bulb, and full headlight assembly replacement when the housing is cracked or too hazed to put out usable light. We fit the bulb type the vehicle calls for and aim the headlights after an assembly swap. We also replace cabin filters that hide behind the glove box, engine air filters, and wiper blades with the correct length and connector.

Pricing, fees, and warranty

Each job is quoted free and in writing, and we get your OK before anything extra. Any diagnostic or trip fee is quoted upfront, which is one reason it makes sense to bundle these with a bigger repair. We take credit, debit, tap to pay, Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, or cash, with no card fees. Filters, bulbs, and blades are wear items, so they aren't covered for normal wear, but our installation is covered under the 30-day labor warranty. See Our Limited Warranty for the full terms.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you do these along with another repair?

Yes, and that's the most common way we do them. Add them to your request and we'll quote everything in one written price.

Do you replace whole headlight assemblies, not just bulbs?

Yes. If the housing is cracked, leaking, or too hazed to light the road properly, we replace the whole assembly.

How often should wipers be replaced?

There's no fixed schedule. Replace them as soon as they streak, chatter, or skip.
