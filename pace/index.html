For a lot of Pace households, getting a car fixed means a drive, a wait, and a second car or a ride to get home. Oilrun removes that step. We bring the shop to your driveway in Pace, do the repair there, and you keep your day. We serve Pace and Pensacola only.

What we do in Pace

We handle brakes and rotors, batteries, alternators and starters, A/C repair, and control arms, struts, and ball joints, as well as check engine lights, tune-ups, radiator and coolant repair, and the filters, bulbs, and wipers that are easy to put off.

Driveway-friendly by design

A flat driveway is ideal. We bring our own jack, jack stands, lighting, and tools, put down protection for any fluids, and clean up when we're done. Check your HOA or apartment rules for repairs in your space. A garage works too if there's room to open the doors and the floor is level.

Quotes, fees, and payment

You get a free written quote before we come out, and if a trip fee or diagnostic fee applies to your visit, it's included in that quote rather than added later. Once we're there, we get your OK before any work beyond the quote. Pay by credit, debit, tap to pay, Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, or cash with no card fees. Our labor carries a 30-day limited warranty. See Our Limited Warranty for the full terms.

What we don't do

We don't offer roadside assistance, standalone oil changes, tire rotations, or fleet accounts. If your vehicle is at home or work and won't start, that's a normal call for us. Our other service area is Pensacola; we don't serve other towns, so if you're unsure, call or text and we'll confirm.
