Oilrun is a mobile mechanic based in Pensacola. Instead of you driving to a shop and arranging a ride, we drive to you with the parts and tools to do the repair where your car is parked: a driveway, an apartment lot, or your employer's parking area, as long as the property allows it.

What we fix in Pensacola

Each service has its own page with symptoms, what we do on-site, and common questions:

  • Brake repair: pads, rotors, and calipers, with a test drive before we leave.
  • Battery replacement: tested first, then replaced with the right group size for your car.
  • Check engine diagnostics: we confirm the cause before you pay for a part.
  • Starters & alternators: no-crank and no-charge problems fixed where the car sits.
  • Tune-ups: plugs, coils, filters, and belts.
  • Steering & suspension: struts, control arms, ball joints, tie rods, and power steering.
  • A/C repair: compressors, condensers, leaks, and recharges done right.
  • Radiator & coolant repair: overheating, leaks, thermostats, hoses, and flushes.
  • Filters, lights & wipers: engine and cabin filters, bulbs, and wiper blades.

How a visit works

Call, text, or send the request form with your year, make, and model and what's going on. We reply with a free written quote and confirm a time by call or text. We show up with the parts, do the repair, test it, clean up, and you pay by credit, debit, or tap to pay. There are no card fees, and if we find anything beyond the quote we stop and get your OK first. Any diagnostic or trip fee is quoted upfront. Our labor carries a 30-day limited warranty. See Our Limited Warranty for the full terms.

Where we go, and what we don't do

We serve the city of Pensacola and also Pace. Not sure whether we cover your spot? Call or text and we'll tell you right away. Check your HOA or apartment rules for repairs in your space. We don't offer roadside assistance, standalone oil changes, or tire rotations. We work at your home or workplace in Pensacola and Pace.
